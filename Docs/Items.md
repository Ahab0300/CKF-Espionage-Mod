#New Items
**Espionage Mod** introduces many new items, available for sale or loot. These items Are
inspired by classic heist and espionage movies. Most are derived from various stealth
Talents in the base game.

Currently all items are available in the **Spy Gear** Service, but some will be moved to
the **MilSec Spy Gear** Service when it is available.

## Buzzcut and Buzzcut Pro
"A quiet buzzer which obscures other sounds."

A tech grenade that creates a zone filled with faint buzzing. **Buzzcut** and the improved
**Buzzcut Pro** reduces the audio detection range of guards and slightly reduces the
detection of gunshots.

##Chameleon Cloak
"A nano-infused cloak which blends with the environment to provide temporary invisibility
while motionless."

When activated, **Chameleon Cloak** renders the user undetectable to sight and sound and grants
some stealth accuracy. Canceled by any movement or attack.

##Holo Disguise
"A holographic disguise to fool visual detection."

When activated, **Holo Disguise** projects the appearance of a local guard, effectively
rendering the user undetectable to other guards and to cameras. Canceled by any attack.

##Ultra Disguise
"A nano-based disguise that fools both visual and Identification Friend or Foe detection."

A more advanced camouflage, **Ultra Disguise** mimics a legitimate bio-signal in addition
to a holographic projection. Other guards, Cameras, Prox-Mines, Bio-Scan Fields and Laser
Wires will not recognize the user. Canceled by any attack.

##E-Pulse, E-Burst, and E-Storm
"A grenade that produces an EMP, jamming both drones and comms."

An Electro-Magnetic Pulse bomb. While jammed by **E-Pulse** or it's more powerful variants,
guards cannot report to the Security AI. Drones cannot report their own death or detect
intruders (while Unaware or Suspicious).

##Lockbreaker Blue, Lockbreaker Red, and Lockbreaker Gold
"A specialized quantum crypto breaker designed to analyze electronic locks to open
Blue Key doors."

A **Lockbreaker** enables a future Leverage to open Blue, Red, or Gold Key doors. Not
available for sale but can be found as loot.

##Mirrorwall
"A portable mirror to block visual detection."

**Mirrorwall** is the classic portable mirror used in so many heist movies.

##Pepper Mist
"A grenade which releases a mild eye irritant, reducing visual range."

A milder form of Pepper Spray, **Pepper Mist** will irritate the eyes and reduce the
visual range of Unaware and Suspicious guards. Has no effect on Drones.

##Rattler
"A noisemaker that lures enemies."

**Rattler** can be thrown to attract the attention of guards.

##Rest-In-Peace
"Device to disable body timers withn an Area of Effect."

The appropriately named **Rest-In-Peace** can be thrown to scramble body timers of
any dead enemies within range.

##SecBuster
"A security breaker that disables both Lvl 1 and Lvl 2 security devices."

The advanced technology of **SecBuster** can turn off those pesky Lvl 2 security devices.

##Snapline and Snapline 007
"Piton gun with a powerful retracting motor, capable of pulling the bearer to a target
 location extremely rapidly."

**Snapline** provides a way to scale platforms and move rapidly, similar to Batman
and James Bond. Move without triggering Overwatch, Security Devices, being seen or
heard while moving and without generating any Recoil. Cannot fire through walls, and
if using Snapline for the second time in a Turn, may generate Injury Time or Stress.

##Spoof-Lite and Spoof-Max
A device to steal the bio-signal of a guard, spoofing the Identification Friend or Foe
system on security devices."

Aim **Spoof-Lite** at an enemy to grab their bio-signal, then use their signal to spoof
many security devices. Will not prevent visual identification.

##Vanishing Act
"A holographic device which projects a burst of confusing images, causing enemies to
lose track of the user."

**Vanishing Act** will change a mercenary from Spotted to Hunted. Do not use while
under observation; it will not prevent enemies from Spotting the user again.
