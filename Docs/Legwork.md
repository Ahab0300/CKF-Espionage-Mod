#New Proc-Gen Legwork Jobs
**Espionage Mod** adds 2 new Proc-Gen Legwork jobs related to spy-work. More Proc-Gen Legwork
jobs may be added later.

##Stakeout Legwork Jobs
"I need Stakeout on a building, Legwork to keep a watch on it for a while."

A squad-member is sent to watch a building. It might be a laboratory, a site of suspicious
criminal activity, a gang meeting place, or a smuggling operation. A good observation post
is critical on a stakeout.

##Surveillance Legwork Job
"I need to put a watch on a person of interest, Legwork doing some old-fashioned
surveillance."

A squad-member is sent to follow a target for several days. The target might be a potential
espionage asset, a criminal suspect, a possible rat or a traitor in the organization, or
the target of an internal investigation. The ability to be inconspicious is important for
surveillance work.
