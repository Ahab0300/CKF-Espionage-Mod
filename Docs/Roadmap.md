#Espionage Mod Roadmap
There are several more things I intend to add to the **Espionage Mod**.

##New Proc-Gen Jobs
I plan at least 5 new Proc-Gen Jobs. The details may change as I learn what is possible
in new Objective types. Some of these objective may not be possible without coding.

###Pilfer Job
A Contact needs to steal some materials. This is a simple mission to visit three
locations to swipe the goods. Mission can be completed with only one site. Stealth is
not required.

###Dead Drop Job
A Contact needs a message left for a mole or agent. This requires visiting one location
to drop the message and visiting a second location to mark the signal informing the agent
that a message is waiting. The trick is that this must be done stealthily. If security
is alerted before both the message and signal are placed, the mission fails.

###Eavesdrop Job
A Contact needs to eavesdrop on a meeting. The squad must get close enough to a VIP -
without being spotted - to throw a self-guiding listening device onto them. If the
bodyguards are alerted before the device is planted, it will be impossible to plant
the device without them noticing and the mission fails.

###Sabotage Job
A Contact wants an opposing operation disrupted. The job is to reach a target location,
spend 3 Turns sabotaging the equipment there, and escape. Stealth is not required.

###Safecracking Job
A Contact wants something stolen from a safe. In classic movie trope form, the safe has
a biometric lock. The squad must get close enough to a VIP to swipe their bio-signature,
then reach the location of the safe.

##New Leverages
At least two new leverages will be added to the mod.

###Patrol Pattern
At start of stage, see the predicted movement of all visible guards.

###Guard Roster
At start of stage, see all guards initially on the map.

##Power Play Mission
There will be a Power Play mission to get the **MilSec Spy Gear** Service.

##Other New Storyline
At least one more espionage storyline will be added to the mod, still TBD.

##Cold War Era
Matsumoto and McKellen enter a cold war, caused by growing tension over the UNA grant
to McKellen to build the new Ward 11. They cannot act directly with the UNA watching
over the project, so it is spy vs. spy. There may be three separate guages in this Era,
two guages measuring the degree of intelligence each side has on the other and a third
measuring the completion of the foundation for the future Ward wall. The concept is
still evolving.
